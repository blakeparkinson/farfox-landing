# Menagerie beanies — November 2026

Approved concepts: waving dark Affirming Antelope; full-body Scan Yak riding an office chair, with SCAN YAK lettering. The original concept previews remain in the main workspace under design/affirming-antelope-beanie/ and design/scan-yak-beanie/.

- Affirming Antelope Beanie: Heather Grey and Navy, one size, $26.
- Scan Yak Beanie: Black and Baby Pink, one size, $26.
- Printful catalog 266, Yupoong 1501KC, flat unlimited-color embroidery on the cuff.
- Production artwork: 1500 × 525 transparent pixels at 300 ppi, matching the 5 × 1.75-inch maximum embroidery area. Keep positioning margins intact.
- The yak lettering is outlined from Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold. Its cap height exceeds the provider's 0.25-inch minimum. The illustrations use full-color embroidery to preserve the approved palettes.

The illustration sources were made with the built-in image-generation tool. Exact prompts are in prompts.json. scripts/make-menagerie-beanies-2026-11.mjs places them at their production size and outlines the type. Matching self-contained SVGs and PNGs are in public/shop/designs/menagerie-beanies-2026-11/. The SVGs embed raster illustrations; they do not imply unlimited detail.

Shop photos are generated by Printful from those production files. They show the actual cuff placement, which is smaller than the earlier concept mockups. Printful performs embroidery digitization when an order is submitted; the digital preview is not a physical stitch sample. Small illustration details may be simplified during digitization.

The publishing script creates only these two named products, skips existing names, and verifies every variant, artwork URL, full-color option and price. It never places an order. Local release-state.json is excluded from the release commit.
