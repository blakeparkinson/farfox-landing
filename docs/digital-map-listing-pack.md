## FAQ

**Is a physical print included?**

No. You receive a high-resolution digital PNG to print wherever you prefer.

**Can I preview it first?**

Yes. The configurator shows your composition before download.

**Can I use any two places?**

Yes. Enter cities, towns, landmarks, or a city plus country for the most reliable match.

**Can I change the wording?**

Yes. Names, short date/detail, and message are customizable within the displayed character limits.

**What sizes can I print?**

The 4:5 file is designed for 8×10, 12×15, and 16×20 output.
