# Holiday gifts Pinterest batch

Articles:
- https://lovefarfox.com/blog/long-distance-relationship-holidays/
- https://lovefarfox.com/blog/long-distance-care-package-ideas/

Run `PINS_FILE=docs/growth/holiday-pins.json node scripts/make-reunion-pins.mjs` to generate five 1000×1500 PNGs into gitignored `out/holiday-pins/`. Titles, descriptions, alt text, and tracked links are in `holiday-pins.json` beside this document. The pins are drafts, not published or scheduled.

Pins take weeks to gain distribution, so publish them by early October if you want them to reach people before the holidays. Space them a few days apart rather than posting all five at once. Campaign: `holiday_gifts_20260925`; each pin has its own `utm_content`.

## Measurement

In Umami, compare site arrivals by `utm_content`, then `blog_map_clicked` and `blog_jersey_clicked` on the two articles (both show the gift offer). These are clicks, not sales: reconcile orders against payment records. After indexing, compare each article's Search Console impressions and query mix over 28 days. Neither article has verified keyword volume or a ranking forecast.
